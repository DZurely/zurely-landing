How Zurely Works

From a confirmed rental to a completed tenancy.

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Enter the tenancy details and review the agreement before generating it.

  2. 02

    Verify & Sign

    Landlord and tenant receive their own secure link to review the draft, complete eKYC and sign independently.

  3. 03

    Stamp

    Once both parties sign, Zurely handles the stamping process and notifies you when stamp duty is ready for payment.

  4. 04

    Done

    Once payment is completed, the stamped tenancy package is delivered to the relevant parties.

About Zurely

Technology should empower agents, not replace them.

Zurely started from a simple observation.

After years working in technology, our founder stepped into Malaysian real estate and experienced the industry from an agent's perspective — where many everyday processes still rely on disconnected tools and manual work.

We saw an opportunity to do things differently. Not by removing the agent from the equation, but by giving them better tools to get the job done.

Tenancy is where we're starting.

  • Our ambition is bigger than tenancy.

  • We're building Zurely towards a future where technology makes real-estate work simpler, more connected and more effective.

  • While keeping property professionals at the centre of the experience.

Your information is protected.

We follow strict data protection practices aligned with Malaysia's PDPA requirements.
