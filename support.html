Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about Zurely, tenancy agreements, stamping, and account security.

What is Zurely and how does it work? General

Zurely is Malaysia's premier digital tenancy compliance workspace built by Double D Sdn. Bhd. It streamlines the entire rental lifecycle for real estate professionals (REN/PEA/REA), landlords, and tenants.

With Zurely, you can generate compliant tenancy agreements in minutes, verify signatories via secure biometric e-KYC, apply legally enforceable digital signatures, automate statutory stamp duty calculation, and receive official stamping documentation in a centralized digital dashboard.

Are Zurely tenancy agreements legally binding in Malaysia? Legal

Yes, absolutely. Agreements generated and signed via Zurely comply strictly with Malaysian law, specifically:

  • Digital Signature Act 1997 (DSA 1997) and Electronic Commerce Act 2006 (ECA 2006) for tamper-evident, legally enforceable electronic execution.
  • Stamp Act 1949: Agreements processed through our stamping pipeline are submitted for official duty assessment with statutory revenue authorities (LHDN), rendering them fully admissible in Malaysian courts.
  • Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) 2010: All biometric and personal identification records are encrypted and protected.
How is stamp duty calculated and how long does stamping take? Stamping

Malaysian stamp duty for tenancy agreements is calculated based on the annual rental amount and the tenancy term in accordance with the Malaysian Stamp Act 1949:

  • 1 Year or less: RM1 for every RM250 (or part thereof) of annual rental exceeding RM2,400.
  • 1 to 3 Years: RM2 for every RM250 (or part thereof) of annual rental exceeding RM2,400.
  • Over 3 Years: RM4 for every RM250 (or part thereof) of annual rental exceeding RM2,400.
  • Duplicate Copies: RM10 per duplicate stamp certificate.

Zurely automatically calculates the exact duty and stepped-rent breakdowns instantly. Once all parties have signed and payment is processed, stamping certificates are typically finalized within 1 to 3 working days.

Does Zurely support multiple parties, companies, and foreign tenants? Tenancy

Yes. Zurely is designed for complex, real-world Malaysian property transactions:

  • Multiple Landlords & Tenants: Add as many joint owners or co-tenants as required.
  • Corporate / Company Entities: Support for Sdn. Bhd., Bhd., Enterprise, Partnerships, and Societies with authorized representative credentials and SSM registration numbers.
  • Foreign National Signatories: International passport verification and foreign nationality support are fully integrated.
How does the e-KYC identity verification work? e-KYC

Before signing any agreement, each party undergoes a quick digital verification step using their smartphone or webcam. This includes scanning their identity document (MyKad or Passport) and completing an AI-powered face liveness check.

This eliminates identity fraud, protects property owners from unauthorized sub-letting, and ensures non-repudiation in legal proceedings.

How do I delete my account and what happens to my data? Account

You can delete your account at any time via Account Settings → Edit Profile → Delete Account in the Zurely app, or by emailing support@zurely.my.

Upon deletion, your login credentials, authentication tokens, profile details, and active preferences will be permanently wiped. In accordance with Malaysian tax and statutory audit requirements (PDPA / LHDN regulations), legally executed agreements and audit logs are securely archived for the mandatory 7-year statutory period.

How do I change my registered phone number or reset my password? Account

To change your registered phone number, navigate to Edit Profile in the app, update the mobile number field, and verify the change via the SMS OTP sent to your new phone number.

If you have forgotten your password, select "Forgot Password" on the login screen, enter your phone number, and complete the SMS OTP verification to set a new password.

Where can I download my stamped agreement and official LHDN certificate? Documents

All completed agreements, official stamping certificates, and payment receipts are stored securely in your Zurely dashboard under the Agreements tab.

You can view, preview, and download individual PDF files or export complete batch packages (including the stamped contract, LHDN receipt, and digital signature audit trail) with a single click.

Account Management & Account Deletion Guide

Learn how to manage your Zurely account, update your credentials, or permanently delete your account.

How to Delete Your Account (In-App Self-Service)

You have full autonomy over your account data. You can delete your account at any time directly through the Zurely mobile or web application.

STEP 01

Open Profile Settings

Open the Zurely App, go to your Account / Profile tab and select Edit Profile.

STEP 02

Scroll to Delete Section

Scroll down to the bottom of the page to find the Delete Account section.

STEP 03

Acknowledge & Confirm

Tick the statutory consent checkbox, tap Delete Account, and confirm the warning dialog.

STEP 04

Verify with SMS OTP

Enter the 6-digit SMS verification code sent to your registered phone number to complete deletion.

Important Notice Regarding Statutory Record-Keeping:
When you delete your account, your personal profile, credentials, and app access are permanently removed. However, pursuant to the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) 2010, the Income Tax Act 1967, and statutory audit standards, all legally executed tenancy agreements, stamping certificates, and official transaction audit logs are retained for seven (7) years for legal non-repudiation and tax compliance.
